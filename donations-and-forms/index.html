Item Donation Form

We want to collect and preserve as much history about Halls Crossroads as possible.  Are you getting ready to move or cleaning out Grandma’s house?  Here are ways you can help us with this goal:

  • We are looking for items made by and/or used by Halls residents – especially those prior to 1960. If you have photographs showing the owners using the item that is a bonus.
  •  If you have a collection of family photographs which you would allow us to scan, we will return those items to you. Pre-1980 pictures would be very helpful.
  • Do have an old family bible with your family’s genealogy?  We would love to make a digital copy to include.
  • Did someone in your family keep scrapbooks?  Would you be willing to donate or loan those to the museum?

We prefer donations to long-term loans but please contact us.  We have limited space at this time for large items.  Please use the forms below or contact us using the links on our Home Page.
